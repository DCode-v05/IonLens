\markboth{DECLARATION}{DECLARATION}

We, Team Astrophage, declare that this research monograph, submitted as part of
our participation in DIA Techathon 2.0 (Naari Shakti) under the thematic domain
\emph{Plasma Technologies for Industry \& Society}, represents our own compilation,
synthesis, and analysis of the scientific literature and of the diagnostic
framework we propose.

All external results, data, and prior work are attributed to their sources through
numbered citations, and every reference listed in the bibliography was located
through direct literature search of public scientific databases. Where physical
values are quoted as ``typical'' or ``representative,'' they are order-of-magnitude
figures used for exposition and are identified as such; they are not presented as
original experimental measurements. The proposed framework is a design and research
proposal; claims of performance are stated as objectives and hypotheses to be
validated through the experimental programme described herein.
